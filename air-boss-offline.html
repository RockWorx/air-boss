Field guide - v3.1

v3.1: a strike jet launches only when fuel (its own plus tanker gas it can actually receive) and weapons support the whole sortie; the rest are held on deck and the readout names the binding resource. The deck is limited per recovery window (about 16 traps in a routine 12-minute window, 29 in a surged 22-minute window) times the events flown, not by a daily wall. Functional check flights and aviator quals fly every day and deliver no effect. The 3-day Strait campaign shows the windowed daily average against a routine target of 120.

Choose a scenario or build a wing. Deck spots are finite. Strike rounds run out into a finite fallback stock. Time-sensitive coordinate targets favor prompt weapons; hardened targets favor heavy warheads.

Yo-Yo fills climb capacity. Strike tankers supply enroute deficits. Consolidation pairs transfer at 150 nm and extend the receiver's reachable orbit. Beyond single-tanker reach, a feasible pair can deliver fuel where a single tanker cannot; inside that reach, the pair costs two deck spots for less daily offload. Both retain return fuel and reserve. Adjust the receiver orbit to explore this boundary. The MQ-25 single reach is 1,400 nm, beyond the 900-nm UI limit, so MQ-25 consolidation cannot win by reach in play. Buddy relay is the teaching case: single reach 212.1 nm versus relay reach 274.2 nm; try a 250-nm orbit. Recovery fuel protects the returning force and cannot supply outbound strikes. Unreachable tracks supply no gas.

The organic cliff is the first 10-nm grid point with less than 90% fuel support, less than half peak effect, or effect below 100. Auto-Boss requires peak effect of 400. Theater fuel incurs a 10% yield penalty and 18% score charge only when accepted. This is an illustrative teaching game, not for planning. Coordinate-delay proxies do not describe real maritime terminal seekers.

How the index works (an abstraction, on purpose). A finite cyclic day sets scheduled sorties at mission range. A few crewed strike sorties are first reserved for functional check flights and aviator quals (no effect). Each remaining strike sortie launches only if it has a weapon (primary rounds first, then a finite fallback bomb stock), a deck recovery slot, and enough fuel of its own plus whole-sortie tanker gas to reach its release distance; otherwise it is held on deck. Departure top-off is capacity-bounded; only launched sorties debit weapons and the one enroute gas pool. Recovery gas is separate. Deck recoveries are bounded per recovery window times the events flown, never by a daily wall.

Effect combines supported sorties, illustrative payload and survival response, air-to-air throughput, strike throughput, target defeat, coordinate validity and recovery service. Theater support changes raw effect by a 10% dogleg yield penalty only when fuel is accepted; an additional 18% dependency charge is displayed separately as score. The attrition line is a separate teaching readout; the 3-day Strait campaign carries magazines, crew fatigue and flight losses from day to day.
