How the index works (an abstraction, on purpose). Each spot generates sorties at 24 ÷ cycle (cycle = out-and-back transit from range + station + deck turn; a bigger/faster-cycling deck and surge tempo shorten the turn). Strikers past ~450 nm need tanker gas (deficit ÷ offload) pooled across the wing — unsupplied strikers don't reach. CAP and ISR/EW spend spots to raise survivability. Effect index = strikes that reach × payload × survivability, summed across the mix, compared to a fixed reference wing. Every coefficient here is fictional and chosen for teaching, not calibrated to real platforms — treat named aircraft as archetypes, not specifications. Not operational analysis and not for planning.
