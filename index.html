Field guide - v3.5

Mission-Governed Launch & Recovery: A strike jet launches only when fuel (its own plus tanker gas it can actually receive), weapons, and a deck recovery slot support the whole sortie; the rest are held on deck and the readout names the binding resource (weapons, fuel, or deck). An unsupplied jet is never launched for zero effect. Deck throughput is limited per recovery window (about 16 traps in a routine 12-minute window, 29 in a surged 22-minute window) times the cyclic events flown, not by a daily wall. Functional check flights and aviator quals fly daily from crewed strike fighters, consuming deck slots and airframes while delivering no combat effect. The 3-day Strait campaign shows the windowed daily average against a routine target of 120.

Deck Spots & Target Matching: Choose a scenario or build a wing. Deck spots are finite (41 on Nimitz, 45 on Ford). Strike rounds run out into a finite fallback stock. Time-sensitive coordinate targets favor prompt Mach 5+ weapons; hardened command bunkers favor heavy penetrators.

Tanker Geometry & Reach: Yo-Yo fills climb capacity. Strike tankers supply enroute deficits. Consolidation pairs transfer at 150 nm and extend the receiver's reachable orbit. Beyond single-tanker reach, a feasible pair can deliver fuel where a single tanker cannot; inside that reach, the pair costs two deck spots for less daily offload. Both retain return fuel and reserve. Adjust the receiver orbit to explore this boundary. The MQ-25 single reach is 1,900 nm in this ledger, beyond the 900-nm UI limit, so MQ-25 consolidation cannot win by reach in play. MQ-25 give is anchored to its published requirement (at least 14,000 lb at 500 nm from the carrier, a threshold and current estimate; demonstrated performance TBD); give at other stations is an illustrative law. Buddy relay is the teaching case: single reach 212.1 nm versus relay reach 274.2 nm; try a 250-nm orbit. Recovery fuel protects the returning force and cannot supply outbound strikes. Unreachable tracks supply no gas.

The Organic Cliff & Auto-Boss: The organic cliff is the first 10-nm grid point with less than 90% fuel support, less than half peak effect, or effect below 100. Auto-Boss requires peak effect of 400. Theater fuel incurs a 10% yield penalty and 18% score charge only when accepted. This is an illustrative teaching game, not for planning. Coordinate-delay proxies do not describe real maritime terminal seekers.

v3.2/v3.4 Logistics Pipeline ("Tempo is a Logistics Bill"): Sortie tempo solves for the daily logistics bill: fuel in gal/day and ordnance in short tons/day. Fuel per sortie rises with sortie duration and standoff radius (+54% from 200 to 500 nm under an illustrative scaling law, anchored roughly at 1,550 gal/sortie at 200 nm); the daily bill also depends on how many sorties fly, so it can fall as the sortie ceiling sags. Standoff distance stretches cyclic events (from 1.75 hr at 200 nm to 2.75 hr at 500 nm), and under this example cycle schedule the daily sortie ceiling can sag by 37.5% (from 120 down to 75 sorties on a 14-hour fly day); real air wings may double-cycle long sorties instead. Combat Logistics Force (CLF) supply shuttles are sized across transit distances (200 to 2,500 nm) to keep deliveries within usable stores and prevent reserve breaches. Aviator flight-time guidelines (daily, 7-day surge, 30-day sustained) and a player-tunable CCA supervisory control ratio (default 4 CCAs per operator) size the operator bill. Reconfiguring stealth fighters for external "beast mode" weapons incurs a mandatory 12-hour deck reset debited against the fly day: on a 14-hour fly day the reset day keeps 2 flying hours (1 event, 15 of 120 sorties); the return to Stealth is not charged in this model.

v3.3/v3.4 "Your Design" Conceptual Aircraft Closure: An unclosed aircraft flies only in PowerPoint. Custom aircraft close only if carrier approach speed (V_PA ≤ 135 kt), high-speed thrust margin (D/T ≤ 0.85), wet-wing fuel volume, and deck spot footprint (Spot ≤ 0.75) close simultaneously. Carrier approach lift is an illustrative assumption (C_L,app = 1.10) standing in for stall-onset and pitch-up limits on uncrewed swept wings, not a validated airworthiness value; it calls for enough wing area to satisfy arrestment limits. Choose Light, Mid, or Heavy dry turbofans with altitude thrust lapse; engine hardware is priced separately from the airframe, with development NRE amortized over fleet buy quantity. Sizing navigates the carrier wing-area pinch: a smaller wing cuts parasite drag but raises induced drag (so high-speed drag has a best wing area), while a larger wing is needed for approach speed and fuel volume. Autonomous CCAs either keep up with the nominal Mach 0.80 strike package or adopt doctrine departures (early launch, push-point loiter, or slower package ingress). Economic readout reports recurring flyaway unit cost, an APUC-style procurement unit cost and a PAUC-style unit cost incl. development (illustrative simplifications: no facilities, no development articles), and cost per delivered combat effect ($/effect, on the PAUC-style basis).

v3.3.1 Guided Tour & CCA Force-Mix Modes: "Watch the Air Boss" features adaptive reading-time pacing scaled to each explainer's word count (holding at least 6 s on Relaxed, 3.6 s on Normal, 2.1 s on Brisk, longer for longer explanations) with transport controls and three CCA tour modes on the same deck: Off flies the pure crewed baseline; CAP fields CCAs in place of crewed CAP fighters (same deck, same strike wing, fewer crew in the air — relieving 28 to 51 cockpit hours a day for defensive cover if one CCA on CAP counts as one crewed CAP fighter, an illustrative assumption); Strike swaps Super Hornets for CCX-1 strikers at 4 aircraft for every 3 deck spots (less payload per spot, fewer crew at risk — an operational choice when human aircrew attrition matters most). How the index works (an abstraction, on purpose). A finite cyclic day sets scheduled sorties at mission range. A few crewed strike sorties are first reserved for functional check flights and aviator quals (no effect). Each remaining strike sortie launches only if it has a weapon (primary rounds first, then a finite fallback bomb stock), a deck recovery slot, and enough fuel of its own plus whole-sortie tanker gas to reach its release distance; otherwise it is held on deck. Departure top-off is capacity-bounded; only launched sorties debit weapons and the one enroute gas pool. Recovery gas is separate. Deck recoveries are bounded per recovery window times the events flown, never by a daily wall.

Effect combines supported sorties, illustrative payload and survival response, air-to-air throughput, strike throughput, target defeat, coordinate validity and recovery service. Theater support changes raw effect by a 10% dogleg yield penalty only when fuel is accepted; an additional 18% dependency charge is displayed separately as score. The attrition line is a separate teaching readout; the 3-day Strait campaign carries magazines, crew fatigue and flight losses from day to day.
