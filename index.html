How the index works (an abstraction, on purpose). Over the day's cyclic events each jet re-flies once per its own cycle (airborne out-and-back + deck turnaround), knocked down by availability and a per-jet cap — about 1.2–1.6 sorties/jet sustained, ~2.6 surge (anchored to the 1997 USS Nimitz surge). Strikers past ~450 nm need tanker gas (deficit ÷ offload) pooled across the wing — unsupplied strikers don't reach. CAP and ISR/EW spend spots to raise survivability; standoff ordnance's survivability edge widens past 400 nm, and the "under fire" line applies attrition (loss/sortie scales with 1 − survivability) to show daily losses and how long the deck sustains. Effect index = strikes that reach × payload × survivability, summed across the mix, compared to a fixed reference wing. Every coefficient here is fictional and chosen for teaching, not calibrated to real platforms — treat named aircraft as archetypes, not specifications. Not operational analysis and not for planning.
